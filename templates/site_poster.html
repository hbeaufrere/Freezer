Dropping off raptor blood samples

Seven steps from the bird to the biobank. Takes about ten minutes after the draw.
{% if site.supplies %}
Supplies:{{ site.supplies }}
{% endif %}
1

Collect the blood sample

Draw blood from the bird into a heparin or EDTA tube. Note the species and the {{ case_number }}.

2

Spin the tube

Centrifuge to separate the plasma from the cells.

3

Plasma into an Eppendorf tube

Pipette the straw-coloured plasma off the top into its own tube.

4

Packed red cells into a second tube

Move the packed RBCs into another Eppendorf tube. Two tubes per bird.

5

Label both tubes

Species and {{ case_number }} on each tube. A Sharpie is fine: Dr. Beaufrère relabels every sample with a printed barcode when it reaches the biobank.

6

Put the tubes in the freezer

Into {{ site.location }}.

7

Scan the code to let Dr. Beaufrère know

Open the camera on your phone, point it at the code and say how many samples you left. No login needed. He gets an email straight away and comes to collect them for the −80 freezer.

Thank you — every sample helps.
