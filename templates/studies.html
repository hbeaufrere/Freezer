Importing a CBC from VMACS
  1. Install Tampermonkey in Chrome, then install the VMACS importer (one click, once per computer).
  2. Open the bird's case in VMACS and press Import CBC → RI study, bottom right of the page. Or copy an email that carries the patient number, open the study here and press From copied email.
  3. Check the panel that comes back, set age and sex, and save. The Excel sheet in the study's folder is rewritten at the same time, once you have linked it.
No Tampermonkey to hand? Every study also takes pasted results and hand entry.
